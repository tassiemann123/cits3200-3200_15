Choosen Project: 3D Skeletal Annotation

Project Description:
The objective is to develop a standalone desktop application for private forensic and bioarchaeological research. The app will accept XYZ coordinates of human skeletal joints and map them onto a realistic 3D human skeleton model. 
This tool will allow researchers to accurately reconstruct, visualise, and analyse the precise positioning of a body within a grave context. Ideally, the app would support importing multiple independent joint datasets simultaneously, 
allowing users to layer, label, and visualise several distinct skeletons within a single shared 3D grave environment. The application must dynamically colour-code skeletons to differentiate individuals within a mass grave with 
automatic and manual override options. To ensure no ongoing licensing fees for this tool, the project must be built using open-source software. The app would require: a simple UI sidebar to manually type XYZ coordinates for major 
skeletal joints, or upload them via a standard CSV file; a 3D viewport featuring a highly detailed, anatomically correct skeleton mesh placed within a simulated grave environment; scripts that translate the inputted spatial coordinates 
to position and articulate the virtual skeleton's joints accurately; full 360-degree orbital rotation, panning, and zooming to allow detailed inspection of the body from any angle; ability to save reconstructions locally and export
 screenshots or 3D view files for research documentation.
